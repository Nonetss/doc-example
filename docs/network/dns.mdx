## What it is

[AdGuard Home](catalogo:services/adguard) is the DNS for the whole home network. It resolves the internal names (`*.home.example`) and forwards the rest to Quad9, stripping ads and tracking.

## Current setup

- It runs in the [docker-01-adguard](catalogo:stacks/docker-01-adguard) stack, on [docker-01](catalogo:machines/docker-01).
- The internal names are DNS rewrites: every `service.home.example` points at `192.168.1.20`.

## Notes

<Callout type="danger">
If docker-01 goes down, the DNS of the whole house goes with it. There is no second DNS.
</Callout>
