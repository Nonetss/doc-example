## The block

```mdx
---
titulo: DNS
descripcion: How the home names are resolved
icono: globe
orden: 2
actualizado: 2026-10-15
machines: [docker-01]
---
```

## The fields every page has

<Entities type="header-fields" columns="id,required,kind,meaning,rule" />

- A page without a valid header is still published, titled with its file name, and its problems are reported.
- Every `icono` of the platform is a Lucide icon name; one Lucide does not have is reported and not drawn.
- `actualizado` is the date of the data: past six months, the page header marks it "Revisar".

## The site's own fields

Any other field must be declared under `cabecera` in [`doc.config.yaml`](../repository/config.mdx), with its kind. A `ref` field (`machines: [docker-01]`) shows its entities as links, and each entity lists the page as one that cites it. An undeclared field is reported.

## The menu wins

When [`menu.yaml`](../repository/menu.mdx) gives a page a title or an icon, that is the one shown everywhere, and a header that disagrees is reported. Give the icons of the pages the menu shows in the menu and leave `icono` out of their headers. `titulo` stays in every header, since it is required: set a title in the menu only when the sidebar needs a different one, and the page then shows the menu's.
