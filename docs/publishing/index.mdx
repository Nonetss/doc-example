## Sync

The platform keeps a clone of every content repository and syncs it:

- at once, on every push to a repository hosted on the platform;
- every five minutes, and when the platform starts;
- when an admin presses **Sincronizar** in `/admin/content`.

Each sync leaves the clone exactly as the branch is. If the remote does not answer, the platform keeps serving the last checkout and `/admin/content` shows the error.

## Validation

Every sync reads the repository in two steps:

1. **The declarations:** `doc.config.yaml`, `menu.yaml` and every `_tipo.yaml`. If any of them is broken, the platform keeps the last valid declarations and logs every problem. Nothing else changes until they are fixed.
2. **The content:** pages, entities and diagrams. Each one is checked on its own; a broken one is reported and the rest is published.

## Where problems show

- **On the page,** to admins only, above its body: the file, the line and what is wrong. Other readers see the page as it could be read.
- **On the repository's home,** to admins: the problems of `menu.yaml`, of the diagrams and of files under `docs/catalogo/`.
- **In the log,** once per commit, with the repository and the file.
